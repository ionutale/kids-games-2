# Khan Academy Kids — Games & Activities Reference

Reference notes on what the Khan Academy Kids app contains: how it organizes content, which
games/activity types are publicly documented, subjects, and age ranges.

- **Prepared:** 2026-09-20
- **Primary sources only:** khanacademy.org / learn.khanacademy.org (official), the official
  Khan Academy Kids Help Center (khankids.zendesk.com, linked from khanacademy.org/kids),
  the official Khan Academy blog (blog.khanacademy.org), and the official App Store /
  Google Play listings by Khan Academy.
- **Scope note:** All claims below link to the official source they came from. Anything not
  stated by an official source is explicitly marked as an inference/caveat.

---

## 1. Bottom line: there is no official exhaustive public list of every game

Khan Academy does **not** publish a complete, itemized public list of every individual game or
activity in the app. The most complete official statement is that the app's **Library "entails
the full scope of our app"** and that its tabs, subheadings, and learning-level dropdowns are the
way to see the full scope and sequence
([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings),
[Help Center: Find books and lessons in the Khan Kids Library](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403657703821-Find-books-and-lessons-in-the-Khan-Kids-Library)).

The closest official breakdowns available to the public are:

1. **Library tabs/sections** — Books, Videos, Create, Offline, Letters, Reading, Math, Logic+
   ([Help Center: Find books and lessons](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403657703821-Find-books-and-lessons-in-the-Khan-Kids-Library)).
2. **Seven learning topics** (reading/literacy, language, math, social-emotional development,
   executive function and logic, motor and physical development, creative expression)
   ([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)).
3. **Character-based content areas** named in the app-store listings (Ollo = phonics/letter
   sounds, Reya = storytime/writing, Peck = numbers/counting, Sandy = puzzles/memory/
   problem-solving) ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/khan-academy-kids/id1378467217),
   [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.khankids.android)), plus the character
   rooms on the home screen ([Help Center: Parent guide](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006764812-Parent-and-Home-Account-Users-Getting-Started-and-creating-an-account-with-Khan-Academy-Kids)).

Individual game names are generally **not** published; see §7 for the handful of activities that
are named or concretely described in official materials, and §8 for caveats.

## 2. How the app organizes content

### 2.1 Three ways to play (home screen)

From the home screen children can ([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)):

- **Personalized Learning Path** — the big green play button; a sequence guided by Kodi the bear
  that "automatically cycles through topics like math, language arts, logic, and social-emotional
  development," surfaced at the child's current level
  ([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings),
  [Help Center: Parent guide](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006764812-Parent-and-Home-Account-Users-Getting-Started-and-creating-an-account-with-Khan-Academy-Kids)).
- **Library** — the purple book icon, top-left; thousands of books, videos, and activities
  ([Help Center: Parent guide](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006764812-Parent-and-Home-Account-Users-Getting-Started-and-creating-an-account-with-Khan-Academy-Kids)).
- **Character rooms** — tap a character at the bottom of the home screen to visit their room,
  play with their toys, and see collections earned by learning
  ([Help Center: Parent guide](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006764812-Parent-and-Home-Account-Users-Getting-Started-and-creating-an-account-with-Khan-Academy-Kids),
  [Help Center: Characters](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049358751-Learn-more-about-the-characters-inside-Khan-Academy-Kids)).

Teachers additionally get in-app **Teacher Tools** (assign lessons, track progress) and a
web-based teacher dashboard
([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/khan-academy-kids/id1378467217),
[Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)).

### 2.2 Library tabs

The Library has tabs "for Books, Videos, Create, Offline, Letters, Reading, Math, and Logic+."
Tabs with a gray dropdown can be filtered by learning level (Preschool through 2nd Grade), and
content is listed under subheadings that require scrolling
([Help Center: Find books and lessons](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403657703821-Find-books-and-lessons-in-the-Khan-Kids-Library),
[Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)).

> Historical note: an older version of the Help Center refers to the math tab as **"123"**
> ("For 123, Reading, and Logic + tabs… select learning levels Preschool through 2nd Grade")
> ([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)).
> The current Help Center uses **Math**. Treat "123" as an earlier name for the math tab.

## 3. Subject areas officially covered

The Help Center lists seven learning topics for ages 2–8, aligned to the Head Start Early
Learning Outcomes Framework and Common Core
([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)):

1. **Reading and literacy** — print knowledge, phonological awareness, alphabet knowledge,
   fiction and nonfiction books, spelling, early writing, and more.
2. **Language** — vocabulary, opposites, prepositions, common objects, verbs, adjectives,
   capitalization, punctuation, sentence types, and more.
3. **Math** — counting, numbers, quantities, addition, subtraction, shapes, measuring, ordering,
   patterns, telling time, number lines, ten frames, and more.
4. **Social-emotional development** — social relationships, emotional development and control,
   empathizing, friendships, and more.
5. **Executive function and logic** — focus, self-control, working memory, flexible thinking,
   problem-solving, and more.
6. **Motor and physical development** — gross and fine motor development, safety, health,
   nutrition, and more.
7. **Creative expression** — drawing, storytelling, recording, self-expression, coloring, and more.

The Help Center also says "the content on Khan Academy Kids covers the foundations early
learners need to develop and grow… thousands of standards-aligned activities about literacy,
language, math, social-emotional development, executive function, physical development, creative
expression and more" (this older article states ages 2–7)
([Khan Academy Help Center: early learners](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360006897372-What-options-does-Khan-Academy-offer-for-early-learners)).

Official subject marketing pages break the two core subjects down further:

- **Early reading & ELA** — pre-reading skills (sounds, rhymes, letters), phonics and decoding,
  vocabulary and language, comprehension (predictions, retelling), writing and spelling
  (tracing, letter formation, word building); book library includes fiction/nonfiction, early
  readers and decodable texts, and longer stories; Read-to-me audio with word highlighting
  ([khanacademy.org/kids/ela](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids/ela)).
- **Math** — counting & number sense, addition & subtraction, place value & base-ten, geometry &
  spatial reasoning, problem-solving & math thinking
  ([khanacademy.org/kids/math](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids/math)).

## 5. Age ranges and leveling

- The app is officially for **children ages 2–8 / preschool through 2nd grade**
  ([khanacademy.org/kids](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids),
  [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/khan-academy-kids/id1378467217),
  [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.khankids.android)).
- The Help Center's map of subjects-to-grade levels:
  - **Preschool** — foundational reading, math, and life skills.
  - **Kindergarten** — phonics, sight words, writing, early math.
  - **1st & 2nd grade** — reading comprehension, problem-solving, confidence
    ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/khan-academy-kids/id1378467217),
    [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.khankids.android)).
- Library tabs can be filtered by **learning level Preschool–2nd Grade**; the Learning Path
  adjusts to the child's performance
  ([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)).
- Some official pages state slightly different ranges: the launch announcement (2018) said
  ages **2–5** ([PR Newswire release by Khan Academy](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/khan-academy-launches-new-educational-program-for-children-ages-two-to-five-300680242.html)),
  and a help article says ages **2–7**
  ([Khan Academy Help Center: early learners](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360006897372-What-options-does-Khan-Academy-offer-for-early-learners)).
  The current official position is **2–8**.
- Books are internally mapped to **Lexile, Atos, and Fountas & Pinnell** levels, but those
  mappings are not shown in the app; book types are instead categorized (character stories,
  social-emotional, early readers, non-fiction animals, alphabet)
  ([Help Center: Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)).

From the official blog post "Free Kindergarten Math Games"
([blog.khanacademy.org/free-kindergarten-math-games](https://blog.khanacademy.org/free-kindergarten-math-games)):

## 8. Caveats about official game lists

1. **No exhaustive public catalog exists.** Khan Academy has not published a list of every game;
   the in-app Library is the authoritative "full scope"
   ([Learning Topics](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014856151-Learning-Topics-Using-Khan-Academy-Kids-in-Educational-Settings)).
   Third-party blog lists should be treated as unofficial.
2. **Counts conflict across official sources** (4,000+ vs 5,000+ vs "thousands" vs 1,000+; see
   §6). Treat all counts as approximate snapshots.
3. **The catalog is dynamic.** Updates add/remove content and seasonal collections are
   time-limited, so any list goes stale quickly
   ([App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/khan-academy-kids/id1378467217)).
4. **Ages shifted over time** — 2–5 at 2018 launch, 2–7 in an older help article, now 2–8.
5. **Some official materials lag the app** (e.g., older "123" tab name vs current "Math" tab).
6. **Everything here is subject to in-app variation.** Content shown may differ by profile age,
   learning level, platform (iOS/Android/Amazon), app version, and parental controls.
