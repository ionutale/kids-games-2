# Camp Khan Kids

- **Type:** Seasonal program / collection
- **Library section:** In-app plus screen-free activities
- **Subject:** Mixed — weekly themes such as arts & crafts, animals, and friendship
- **Levels:** Not stated
- **Sources:** [Camp Khan Kids official page](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids/camp),

## Description

Camp Khan Kids is an annual free virtual summer camp with in-app and screen-free activities,
organized around weekly themes such as arts & crafts, animals, and friendship.

## Notes

- Seasonal and time-limited; themes and dates vary by year.
