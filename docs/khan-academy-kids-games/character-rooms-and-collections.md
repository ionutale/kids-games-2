- **Type:** Interactive areas (home screen, not the Library)
- **Library section:** —

- **Levels:** All app levels

## Description

Tapping a character at the bottom of the home screen opens their room, where children play with
the character's toys and see collections earned by learning. As children complete lessons they
collect bugs, hats, toys, and clothes. Officially noted room details include Kodi's hats (a hat
collection), Ollo's bath toys, Reya's bug collection, Peck's dress-up closet, and Sandy's musical
instruments.
