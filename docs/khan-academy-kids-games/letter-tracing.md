## Description

Letter tracing is letter-formation practice inside Ollo's ABC tab, in the Tracing section. Ollo
the elephant is the character tied to phonics and letter sounds. The Letters tab overall offers
hundreds of phonics lessons covering every letter of the alphabet — lowercase and uppercase
letters, letter sounds, vowel sounds, and CVC words — for ages 3–5.
