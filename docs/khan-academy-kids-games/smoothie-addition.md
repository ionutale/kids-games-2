# Make smoothies by adding

- **Type:** Math activity
- **Library section:** Math
- **Subject:** Math — addition
- **Levels:** Kindergarten (the blog presents it among Kindergarten math games)
- **Source:** [Khan Academy Blog — Free Kindergarten Math Games](https://blog.khanacademy.org/free-kindergarten-math-games)

## Description

An early addition activity: the bear in the corner tells kids to drag 2 oranges and one strawberry
into the blender to make a smoothie.
