- **Type:** Book reading mode

## Description

Read To Me is the read-aloud mode for books in the Khan Kids Library: the app reads the book
aloud in English or Spanish. Official materials describe read-to-me audio with word highlighting,
so children can follow along as words are spoken.

## Notes

- Word highlighting is described on the official early-reading page.
