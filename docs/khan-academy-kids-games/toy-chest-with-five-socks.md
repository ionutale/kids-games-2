# Find the toy chest with five socks

- **Type:** Math activity
- **Library section:** Math
- **Subject:** Math — counting and number sense (subitizing)
- **Levels:** Kindergarten (the blog presents it among Kindergarten math games)
- **Source:** [Khan Academy Blog — Free Kindergarten Math Games](https://blog.khanacademy.org/free-kindergarten-math-games)

## Description

A subitizing activity: as children become more familiar with numbers, they quickly choose the
drawer that has 5 socks — without counting.
